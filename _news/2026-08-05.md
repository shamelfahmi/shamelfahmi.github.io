:fire: Bicycle robots are incredibly fast but hard to control. In our recent work, we show that Reinforcement Learning (RL) can solve this issue and unlock a diverse repertoire of dynamic acrobatic behaviors.

Our Ultra Mobility Vehicle (UMV) autonomously learns front and lateral table jumps, front flips, kip-ups and kip-downs, wheelies, bunny hops, three-point turns, and long-horizon compositions of these skills through a policy orchestrator; all with RL.
